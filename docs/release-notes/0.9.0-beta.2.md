# TEKSO 0.9.0 beta 2

[Български](../bg/release-notes/0.9.0-beta.2.md) · [Docs index](../README.md) · [Previous: beta 1](0.9.0-beta.1.md)

Free during the beta.

The second beta adds scanned-PDF text recognition (OCR), drawing and filling on PDF pages, many more file formats you can open, Office templates, and a large round of fixes found by testing on real documents.

## Highlights

- **OCR for scanned PDFs.** Recognize text on a scan and add an invisible text layer, so the page can be searched and copied. The recognition models are bundled and run on your computer, nothing is uploaded. Bulgarian and other Cyrillic text is supported. A banner offers it when a PDF looks like a scan.
- **PDF on the page.** Draw and fill directly on the page, place a signature field, compare two PDFs, and use an accessibility checklist with tagging in the interface. A truncated PDF is now recovered when possible, and text inside form objects is read.
- **More formats to open.** Older PowerPoint (`.ppt`), flat OpenDocument (`.fodt`, `.fods`, `.fodp`), AbiWord (`.abw`), RTF with attachments (`.rtfd`), Kindle books (`.mobi`, `.azw3`), Hancom (`.hwpx`), and Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote files. They are imported, and a copy is saved in an open format. Compatibility is best effort.
- **Office templates.** `.dotx`, `.xltx`, `.potx` (and the macro variants) open as a new untitled document, with macros removed and reported. You can save your own template to your templates folder, start a new document from a template file, and list your templates.
- **Spreadsheets and charts.** Drag to fill and to reorder, scatter charts, floating charts you can move and remove, point-and-click formula references, a comment balloon, and in-place galleries for charts, tables, shapes and colors.
- **Text.** Apply a table style as a "look", copy a text format from a table style, and a separator for notes taken from the file. Tracked-change accept and reject got a set of adversarial tests.
- **Fewer dialogs.** 24 single-field commands now ask in a small balloon at the place of the change, and 14 more let you choose the place directly.
- **Claude panel.** A Stop button and a confirmation step.
- **Publications.** `.opub` documents are saved automatically and offered for recovery. The recovery copy is removed when you save.
- **macOS.** System Services and recent documents in the Dock.
- **AI connector.** 427 tools, up from 423 in beta 1: PDF OCR, image OCR, and moving or removing charts, among others. Tools are now labelled read-only or destructive.

## Fixes

- Files saved by tools that use Info-ZIP no longer fail to save.
- Spreadsheet sheet names with a trailing space.
- PDF: Cyrillic text with unusual glyph names, text placed outside the page, unreadable CAD junk, and much lower memory use when reading text.
- Protection against PDF "decompression bombs" (a single stream is capped at 256 MiB).
- The AI connector no longer opens the whole disk when no folder is set, does not overwrite someone else's file without `overwrite`, writes files atomically, and validates calls and long errors.
- Windows: long and `\\?\` paths in the AI connector, and the OCR models are copied and checked by the installer build.
- Replacing an OCR layer no longer grows the file by a whole embedded font.
- Many interface and Bulgarian wording fixes, plural forms in both languages.

## Known issues

- OCR accuracy was measured on synthetic pages only. It has not been measured on real scans, and on large scans OCR can use more than 2 GB of memory.
- Apple formats are read, not written. A spreadsheet saved by TEKSO may show no tables in Apple Numbers (not confirmed).
- Not yet checked by hand in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Acrobat for the new features. Files may look different, especially complex ones. Keep backups.
- No Intel Mac build yet.
- No real-time collaboration, no macros, no live multi-monitor presenting.
- No screen-reader support yet.
- Arabic text in PDF/A is limited.
- Not yet tested with every smart card and in every version of Claude Desktop.

# TEKSO 0.9.0 beta 1

[Български](../bg/release-notes/0.9.0-beta.1.md) · [Docs index](../README.md)

The first public beta. Free during the beta.

## Highlights

- One app for text, spreadsheets, presentations and PDF.
- Opens and saves `.docx`, `.xlsx` and `.pptx`. Reads `.odt`, `.ods` and `.odp`.
- PDF: editing, forms, PDF/A, accessibility checks, and signing with a qualified electronic signature (QES/КЕП) on a smart card.
- Optional AI help through MCP. It is off by default, every change is a proposal you accept or reject, and passwords and PINs never reach the AI.
- English and Bulgarian interface, light and dark themes and five color themes.
- Local only: no account, no cloud, no telemetry, no automatic update checks.
- Version history next to your documents and automatic recovery copies.

## Platforms

- macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon.
- Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. A per-user **.exe** installer and an **.msi** for administrators.

The installers are not code-signed. See [Install](../install.md).

## Known issues

- No Intel Mac build yet.
- No real-time collaboration, no macros, no live multi-monitor presenting.
- No screen-reader support yet.
- Arabic text in PDF/A is limited.
- Files may look different in Microsoft Office, especially complex ones. Keep backups.
- Not yet tested with every smart card and in every version of Claude Desktop (to be confirmed).
